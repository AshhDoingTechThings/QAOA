Problem statement

Scarce resources, many hospitals, hard constraints

Hospitals must share limited beds, staff, equipment and funds while respecting demand, capacity, cost, priority, fairness and minimum service.

The number of possible allocations grows exponentially: with 2 qubits per facility, 10 facilities already give 220 ≈ 106 bit-strings. Manual or rule-based planning is slow and often sub-optimal.

Engineer's noteMost QAOA papers solve unconstrained toy problems (MaxCut) and never compare against classical solvers fairly. This framework encodes real constraints, solves the same instance classically and with QAOA, and reports quality, feasibility, runtime, stability, qubits and depth. We do not claim quantum advantage.

Step 2 · Mathematical model → QUBO

One binary objective

Decision variables. Each facility gets a 2-bit register:

af = xf,0 + 2·xf,1 ∈ {0,1,2,3},   x ∈ {0,1}

Objective + penalties:

minx xTQx = Hcost + λ1Hcapacity + λ2Hdemand + λ3Hfairness + λ4Hmin-service
  • Hcost = Σ cfaf
  • Hdemand = Σ pf·max(0, df−af): unmet demand weighted by priority
  • Hcapacity = Σ max(0, af−capf) + (Σaf + s − S)²: slack s turns Σa ≤ S into an equality
  • Hfairness = Σf<g(af/df − ag/dg)²: equal coverage ratios
  • Hmin = Σ max(0, mf−af)
Engineer's noteAny function of one 2-bit register is exactly quadratic: g(a) = g₀ + (g₁−g₀)x₀ + (g₂−g₀)x₁ + (g₃−g₂−g₁+g₀)x₀x₁. So the piece-wise “max(0,·)” terms need no extra ancilla qubits.

Step 3 · Ising mapping

From bits to spins

xi = (1 − zi) / 2,   zi ∈ {−1,+1}
HC = Σi hiZi + Σi<j JijZiZj + const

Each qubit is drawn as a Bloch sphere. Each coupling Jij is a bond whose thickness shows |J| and whose colour shows its sign. Each ZiZj term becomes an RZZ gate (2 CNOTs) in every QAOA layer.

Watch the circuit act on the qubits

After a QAOA run, step through the layers. The cost layer is diagonal: it changes phases and rotates the arrows around Z. The mixer then turns those phase differences into changes in amplitude.

Step 6 · Benchmarking

Transparent, reproducible comparison

Gap = (Csolver − Copt) / Copt × 100
r = Copt / CQAOA  ·  Popt = shotsopt / shots
  • Stability is the standard deviation across seeds and initial angles.
  • Feasibility rate is the share of measured shots that satisfy every constraint.
  • Depth / CX come from the Qiskit transpiler, with all-to-all and linear connectivity.
Engineer's noteRead the numbers honestly. On instances this small, exact enumeration and annealing are faster and more reliable. QAOA's value here is a measured, reproducible baseline: how feasibility and Popt grow with depth p, and how noise and the CX count grow with it. That shows when hardware would need to improve before QAOA becomes competitive.
